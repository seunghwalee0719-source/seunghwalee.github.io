Our approach

Electrifying chemical engineering, from catalyst to working interface.

SEE Lab builds on the former EC-Lab philosophy of catalyst synthesis, system engineering, and in-situ/operando analysis—now with a stronger focus on dynamic interfaces and durability under realistic electrochemical conditions.

01
Catalyst design

Control composition, structure, active phases, and reconstruction for efficient electrochemical reactions.

02
System & interface engineering

Connect catalyst behavior with electrolytes, transport, ionomers, electrodes, and practical electrolyzer operation.

03
Operando analysis

Use real-time spectroscopy and electrochemistry to understand what working catalysts actually become under bias.

Research themes

Four connected directions.

H₂

Water Electrolysis

Acidic and alkaline OER/HER catalysis for efficient, durable hydrogen production.

Δ

Dynamic Catalysts

Reconstruction, oxidation state, dissolution, and the evolution of active phases during operation.

OP

Operando Spectroscopy

Raman, X-ray absorption, infrared, and complementary tools for real-time interface analysis.

CO₂

Molecular Conversion

Electrochemical reaction engineering for carbon and energy conversion, informed by interfacial chemistry.
