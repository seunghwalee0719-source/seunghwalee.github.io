Our approach

From interfacial chemistry to durable electrocatalysis.

SEE Lab combines catalyst design with operando and in-situ characterization to understand how active materials evolve under realistic electrochemical conditions.

✓
Electrocatalyst design

Structure, composition, and reconstruction of catalysts for water electrolysis and related reactions.

✓
Operando interface analysis

Raman, X-ray absorption, infrared, and electrochemical tools to track working catalysts.

✓
Stability engineering

Linking dissolution, oxidation state, surface reconstruction, and activity to long-term durability.

Research themes

Three connected research directions.

01

Electrocatalysis

Acidic and alkaline water electrolysis, catalyst reconstruction, active-site control, and durability.

02

Operando Spectroscopy

Watching catalysts evolve during reaction through Raman, XAS, SEIRAS, and complementary methods.

03

Interface Engineering

Understanding ionomer, water, oxygen, electrolyte, and electrode interactions at dynamic interfaces.
