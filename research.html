Research

Electrochemical interfaces in motion.

We investigate how catalysts transform during operation and use that knowledge to engineer more active and durable electrochemical systems.

H₂

Water Electrolysis

Acidic and alkaline OER/HER catalysts, stability–activity relationships, metal dissolution, and reconstructed active phases.

OP

Operando Characterization

In-situ Raman, X-ray absorption spectroscopy, SEIRAS/SERS, and electrochemical diagnostics under working conditions.

IF

Electrochemical Interfaces

Interfacial water, electrolyte ions, ionomers, oxygen transport, catalyst surfaces, and their coupling during reaction.

Scientific question

What is the catalyst really doing under bias?

Ex-situ structures are only snapshots. Our central interest is the evolving working interface: oxidation state, coordination, surface phase, lattice oxygen participation, dissolution, and transport.

1
Observe

Track structural and chemical changes during electrochemical operation.

2
Correlate

Relate spectroscopic signatures to activity, selectivity, and degradation.

3
Engineer

Use mechanistic insight to design interfaces that remain active for longer.
