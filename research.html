Research

What changes at the electrochemical interface?

Our research follows catalysts while they work—connecting synthesis, electrochemical performance, structural evolution, and interfacial chemistry to engineer more sustainable reactions.

01
Catalyst design

Electrocatalysts for sustainable energy conversion

We design and synthesize catalysts for water electrolysis and related electrochemical energy-conversion reactions. Rather than treating the as-synthesized material as a fixed structure, we consider how composition, defects, phase, and reconstruction determine the working catalyst.

Acidic OERAlkaline OERHEREarth-abundant catalystsPrecious-metal catalysts
02
Systems

Electrolyzers and electrochemical engineering

Catalysts ultimately operate inside electrodes and devices. We connect intrinsic catalyst chemistry with electrolyte composition, transport, ionomer–catalyst contact, electrode architecture, and long-term operation to understand performance at practical conditions.

Water electrolysisAEM systemsElectrode architectureTransportStability testing
03
Operando

Real-time spectroscopy of working interfaces

In-situ and operando measurements help us identify oxidation-state changes, coordination environments, surface intermediates, and reconstructed phases that cannot be understood from ex-situ characterization alone.

RamanX-ray absorptionSEIRAS / SERSElectrochemical diagnosticsInterface analysis
04
Mechanism & durability

From reconstruction to degradation

We study how activity and degradation emerge together. Dissolution, lattice participation, oxidation-state cycling, electrolyte impurities, and structural reconstruction are treated as coupled processes that can be observed and ultimately engineered.

Catalyst reconstructionDissolutionActive oxygenImpurity effectsLong-term durability
Research logic

Observe → understand → engineer.

ObserveFollow the catalyst and interface under electrochemical bias.
UnderstandRelate spectroscopic and structural changes to kinetics and degradation.
EngineerDesign materials, interfaces, and operating conditions that remain active for longer.
