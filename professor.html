Principal Investigator

Seunghwa Lee

Sustainable Electrochemical Engineering Lab
Sookmyung Women’s University

Our research focuses on understanding dynamic electrochemical interfaces and translating mechanistic insight into durable catalysts and sustainable energy-conversion technologies. We combine electrocatalyst design with operando and in-situ spectroscopy to investigate how catalytic materials reconstruct, activate, and degrade under realistic reaction conditions.

Research Interests

Research at the working interface.

01

Electrocatalysis

Oxygen evolution, hydrogen evolution, catalyst reconstruction, dissolution, and durability.

02

Operando Spectroscopy

Raman, X-ray absorption spectroscopy, SEIRAS/SERS, and complementary electrochemical analysis.

03

Electrochemical Interfaces

Interfacial water, electrolyte ions, catalyst surfaces, ionomers, and transport phenomena.

04

Sustainable Energy

Mechanism-driven materials design for water electrolysis and electrochemical energy conversion.

Research Philosophy
“SEE” the interface first — then engineer the reaction.

We aim to connect spectroscopy, electrochemistry, and materials chemistry so that catalyst design is guided by what actually happens under operating conditions.
