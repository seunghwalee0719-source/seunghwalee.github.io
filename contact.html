Join us

Curious about electrochemistry? Get in touch.

We welcome inquiries from students interested in undergraduate research, graduate study, and research internships. SEE Lab works on sustainable electrochemical energy conversion through catalyst synthesis, device and interface engineering, and in-situ/operando analysis.

Prior experience in electrochemistry is not a strict requirement. A strong willingness to learn, careful experimental practice, and curiosity about how materials behave under reaction conditions are highly valued.
